Common Questions

What Homeowners Ask

Do you give upfront pricing before starting work?

Yes. We assess the job, explain what needs to be done, and give you a clear cost before we touch anything. No invoices that surprise you after the fact.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. Griggs Electric holds Texas Electrical Contractor License #35694. All work is performed by licensed electricians and we carry full liability and workers' comp coverage.

How quickly can you schedule a project?

Because we focus exclusively on a micro-regional service area, we typically have much tighter availability windows than larger contractors. Call us and we'll give you real timing — not vague estimates.

Can you work alongside my contractor on a remodel?

We do it often. Griggs Electric coordinates directly with GCs, builders, and designers to keep your project timeline intact. We know how to work in a busy job site.

Do you install EV chargers?

Yes — Level 2 home EV charger installation is one of our most popular services. We assess your panel capacity, run the appropriate circuit, and have your charger operational quickly.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Trophy Club, Southlake, Westlake, Keller, Roanoke, Northlake, Justin, Argyle, Alliance, Lantana, Grapevine, and Haslet. Our focused footprint means better service for you.
